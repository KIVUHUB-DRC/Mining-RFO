02 / SPECTRAL MEASURES

Six indices, complementary signals

Indices are derived from atmospherically corrected Sentinel-2 surface reflectance. Directional descriptions below explain screening signals; they do not establish cause.

NDVI(NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red)

Vegetation greenness. A sharp decrease can indicate vegetation removal or stress.

EVI2.5 × (NIR − Red) / (NIR + 6Red − 7.5Blue + 1)

Vegetation response with atmospheric and background adjustments.

NBR(NIR − SWIR₂) / (NIR + SWIR₂)

Vegetation/moisture contrast; also responds to burn scars and other disturbance.

NDMI(NIR − SWIR₁) / (NIR + SWIR₁)

Canopy and vegetation moisture response.

BSI((SWIR₁ + Red) − (NIR + Blue)) / sum

Relative bare-soil signal; may respond to roads, fields or natural exposed ground.

MNDWI(Green − SWIR₁) / (Green + SWIR₁)

Water-sensitive measure used to help avoid some water-body changes.

03 / SCREENING RULE

What would trigger a candidate flag?

The reproducible script requires a simultaneous, multi-index pattern on valid 20 m pixels:

ΔNDVI ≤ −0.18VEGETATION LOSS
ΔBSI ≥ +0.10BARE-SOIL INCREASE
ΔNBR ≤ −0.16CHANGE SIGNAL
ΔNDMI ≤ −0.08MOISTURE DECREASE
  • Current BSI must be positive and NDVI no greater than 0.65.
  • MNDWI must be no greater than 0.15 as a conservative water-change exclusion.
  • Connected patches smaller than 0.5 ha are excluded.
  • Every geometry and statistic is intended to be clipped to the exact RFO boundary.

Important: These thresholds flag land-cover change for review; they are not validated mine/no-mine decision limits. A flagged patch could be agriculture, logging, river dynamics, landslide, fire, shadow or another exposed surface.

05 / CAVEATS

What this assessment cannot claim

!
No confirmed mining sites

The site inventory is empty because a complete run was not produced. This is not a verified zero.

  • The raster job did not finish across the whole reserve and all analysis periods.
  • Sentinel-1 radar, Landsat, NICFI, GLAD/GFW, Hansen Global Forest Change, FIRMS, and field/patrol observations were not used to verify candidate locations.
  • No authoritative historical mine inventory, patrol-post layer, or management-sector layer was provided.
  • Catalog-level scene counts include imagery with varying cloud and overlap; they are not usable-pixel counts or mining metrics.
  • Small, short-lived, cloud-obscured, canopy-covered or spectrally subtle activity may not be detected at 20 m.

The correct result for the current run is “not determined”—not “no mining.”
